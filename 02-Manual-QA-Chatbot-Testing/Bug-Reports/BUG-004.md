# BUG-004 — Requested Product Replaced During Comparison

**Environment:** Web application, manual testing

**Severity:** High
**Priority:** High
**Status:** Open

### Steps to Reproduce

1. Open the chatbot.
2. Ask the chatbot to compare two specific products.
3. Request a comparison between **iPhone 16** and **Samsung S24**.
4. Review the products included in the chatbot's comparison.

### Actual Result

The chatbot replaces **Samsung S24** with **Samsung Galaxy S26** in the comparison.

### Expected Result

The chatbot should compare the exact products requested by the user: **iPhone 16** and **Samsung S24**.
