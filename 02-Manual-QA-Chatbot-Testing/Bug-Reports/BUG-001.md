## Summary

The product price displayed by the AI e-commerce chatbot does not match the price shown on the corresponding product page.

The chatbot displayed **1999 GEL**, while the linked product page displayed **2099 GEL** for the same product.

This creates a product-information consistency issue and may affect customer purchase decisions.

---

## Environment

* **Application:** Confidential AI-Powered E-commerce Chatbot
* **Platform:** Web
* **Testing Approach:** Manual Testing
* **Testing Type:** Functional / Exploratory
* **Browser:** Web browser
* **Environment:** Available test environment

> Exact browser version and execution date are not documented because they were not recorded during the original test session.

Before starting the test:

1. The chatbot is available.

4. The chatbot can provide a product link.

## Steps to Reproduce

1. Open the AI e-commerce chatbot.
2. Start a product-search conversation.
3. Ask the chatbot to recommend a laptop.
4. Specify a requirement of **16 GB RAM**.
5. Specify a requirement of **512 GB storage**.
6. Ask the chatbot to provide a suitable product.
7. Record the product price displayed by the chatbot.
8. Open the product link provided by the chatbot.
9. Locate the price displayed on the product page.
10. Compare the chatbot price with the product-page price.

---

## Expected Result

The chatbot should display the same current product price as the corresponding product page.

If the chatbot cannot guarantee real-time pricing, it should clearly inform the user that the displayed price may differ from the current product-page price.

---

## Actual Result

The chatbot displayed a price of:

**1999 GEL**

The linked product page displayed:

**2099 GEL**

The prices did not match.

---

## Impact

This issue may:

* provide customers with incorrect pricing information
* create confusion during the purchase process
* lead users to expect an unavailable price
* reduce confidence in the chatbot's product information
* potentially influence purchase decisions based on incorrect data

Because price is a critical product attribute, the issue was assigned **High Priority**.

---

## Severity

**Medium**

The issue does not prevent the user from accessing the product, but it provides incorrect customer-facing information.

---

## Priority

**High**

Pricing information directly affects purchase decisions and should be corrected promptly.

---

## Reproducibility

**Reproducible during manual testing.**

The mismatch was observed by comparing the chatbot response with the linked product page.

---

## QA Assessment

The issue is classified as a **data consistency / functional defect** because two customer-facing parts of the product experience provide different information for the same product.

The defect should be investigated before considering the chatbot product-information flow fully reliable.

---

## Recommendation

Possible corrective approaches include:

1. Synchronize chatbot product information with the current product data source.
2. Ensure the chatbot retrieves the current product price when generating recommendations.
3. Validate product information before presenting it to the customer.
4. If real-time synchronization is not possible, display a clear disclaimer that the price may differ from the current product page.
5. Include product-price consistency in regression testing after the fix.
