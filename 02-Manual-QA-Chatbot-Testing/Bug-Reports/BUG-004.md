# BUG-004 — Product Replacement During Comparison

## Summary

The chatbot replaces one of the products requested by the user with a different product during a comparison without asking for confirmation.

## Environment

* Application: Confidential AI-Powered E-commerce Chatbot
* Platform: Web
* Browser: Web browser
* Testing Type: Manual / Exploratory Testing

## Preconditions

* Chatbot is available.
* Product comparison functionality is available.
* The user can request a comparison between two products.

## Test Data

* Product A
* Product B

## Steps to Reproduce

1. Open the chatbot.
2. Enter the request: `Compare Product A and Product B`.
3. Wait for the chatbot response.
4. Review the products used in the comparison.
5. Check whether the chatbot compares the exact products requested by the user.

## Expected Result

The chatbot should compare the exact products requested by the user.

If one of the requested products is unavailable, the chatbot should:

1. Inform the user that the product is unavailable.
2. Ask whether the user wants to compare another available product.
3. Replace the product only after receiving user confirmation.

## Actual Result

The chatbot compares Product A with a different product instead of the requested Product B.

The replacement happens without asking the user for confirmation.

## Severity

**Medium**

## Priority

**High**

## Impact

* Users may receive incorrect comparison information.
* Users may make decisions based on products they did not request.
* The behavior can reduce trust in chatbot recommendations.
* Product comparison accuracy is affected.

## Reproducibility

**Reproducible during manual testing**

## Recommendation

The chatbot should never silently replace a requested product.

If a requested product is unavailable, the chatbot should clearly communicate the issue and ask the user whether they want to compare an alternative product.
