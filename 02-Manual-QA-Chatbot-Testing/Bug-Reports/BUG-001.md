# BUG-001 — Product Price Mismatch

**Environment:** Web application, manual testing

**Severity:** Medium
**Priority:** High
**Status:** Open

### Steps to Reproduce

1. Open the chatbot.
2. Ask for a laptop with specific requirements.
3. Select or receive a recommended product.
4. Note the price provided by the chatbot.
5. Open the corresponding product page.
6. Compare the prices.

### Actual Result

The chatbot displays **1999 GEL**, while the product page displays **2099 GEL**.

### Expected Result

The price displayed by the chatbot should match the price shown on the corresponding product page.

### Related Test Case

Product price validation

### Related Test Scenario

Product information and price consistency
