# BUG-001 — Product price in chatbot does not match the product page

## Title

Chatbot shows a different product price than the linked product page

## Environment

- Platform: Alta AI Chatbot
- Browser: Web browser
- Testing type: Manual / Exploratory Testing

## Preconditions

- Chatbot is available.
- User has selected a laptop based on the chatbot's recommendations.

## Steps to Reproduce

1. Ask the chatbot about laptops.
2. Ask for a laptop with 16GB RAM and 512GB SSD.
3. Ask the chatbot to show the selected product.
4. Open the product link provided by the chatbot.
5. Compare the price shown in the chatbot with the price on the product page.

## Actual Result

The chatbot stated that the product price was **1999 GEL**.

After opening the product link, the actual product page showed a price of **2099 GEL**.

The price provided by the chatbot does not match the price displayed on the product page.

## Expected Result

The chatbot should display the same current price as the product page.

If the price has changed, the chatbot should provide the updated price or clearly inform the user that the price may have changed.

## Severity

Medium

## Priority

High

## Impact

The user may make a purchase decision based on incorrect price information.

This can create confusion and reduce trust in the chatbot and the shopping process.

## Evidence

The chatbot provided a product link and displayed a price of 1999 GEL, while the linked product page displayed 2099 GEL.
