# BUG-002 — Cart Total Price Does Not Update When Quantity Is Changed

**Severity:** High
**Priority:** High
**Status:** Reported
**Environment:** Web Application
**Module:** Shopping Cart

## Description

The cart total price does not change when the product quantity is updated.

## Steps to Reproduce

1. Open the e-commerce web application.
2. Add a product to the shopping cart.
3. Open the shopping cart.
4. Change the product quantity.
5. Check the cart total price.

## Expected Result

The cart total price should be updated according to the selected product quantity.

## Actual Result

The cart total price does not change after the product quantity is updated.
